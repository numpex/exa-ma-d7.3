\subsubsection{Analysis of Survey Responses}

The survey data gathered for Exa-MA Master tracks was analyzed over several dimensions. 
\Cref{fig:bar-chart} presents a grouped bar chart displaying the distribution of course levels (categorized as \emph{None}, \emph{Introduction}, \emph{Intermediary}, and \emph{Advanced}) across 12 HPC-related topics. In general, the results show a strong emphasis on advanced and introductory courses for topics such as High Performance Computing and Discretization for PDEs, whereas topics like Reduced Order Methods received far fewer course offerings.

\Cref{fig:training-format} and~\cref{fig:contact-preferences} summarize two survey questions using pie charts. 
The training format responses reveal a clear preference for a combined approach that integrates both project-based learning and internships. 
In addition, nearly all respondents indicated that they wish to receive further communications from Exa-MA regarding internships, semester projects, or PhD proposals, demonstrating a high level of engagement with the program.

\textbf{Analysis:}  
The data shows an emphasis on practical, hands-on experiences.
Several students from Master CSMI at Unistra have used internships to transition into PhD programs.
Students from other institutions, such as Master Applied Math Nice and Master IA Saclay, also participate in collaborative projects.
Some training experiences at University of Pau and Pays de l’Adour are still in progress.
Overall, the outcomes suggest a clear connection between academic learning and applied research.
These experiences help prepare students for research opportunities within the Exa-MA framework and more generally in the field of computational science.
