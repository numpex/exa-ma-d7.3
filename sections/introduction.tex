\subsection{Context and Objectives}

The Exa-MA project (\textit{Méthodes et Algorithmes à l'Exascale}) is a central part of the national PEPR NumPEx program. Its goal is to drive breakthrough research and develop software that brings exascale-level computing to a wide range of scientific and industrial fields. Exa-MA is all about pushing the limits of numerical methods and algorithms, focusing on overcoming key exascale challenges (B1--B13) such as reducing communication overhead, managing diverse hardware, ensuring resilience, and handling data-heavy processes.

Within Exa-MA, \textbf{Work Package 7 (WP7)} makes sure that these innovative exascale solutions reach everyone who can benefit from them. \textbf{Task 7.4 (Training)} is specifically responsible for creating, organizing, and sharing training materials. These resources are meant to help both newcomers and experienced users get up to speed with Exa-MA's latest methods and software tools.

\textbf{Deliverable D7.3} gives an \textit{initial} look at the training materials and plans for the project. In this document, you'll find:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Our overall \textit{training strategy} (see Section~\ref{sec:strategy}) aimed at equipping different audiences with exascale computing skills.
    \item A snapshot of the \textit{current training materials} (see Section~\ref{sec:current}), which includes tutorials, workshops, and details on academic collaborations.
    \item Our plans for future improvements to these materials to keep up with the evolving needs of Exa-MA (see Section~\ref{sec:next-steps-software}).
\end{itemize}

We'll update this document at future milestones (M24, M36, \dots) as new developments and feedback come in from our ongoing work.

\subsection{Background and Motivation}

The push toward exascale computing comes with big opportunities and some tough challenges. Traditional numerical methods and algorithms aren't always ready for the scale and complexity of modern systems. Exa-MA was started to address these challenges by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Boosting Performance:} Finding new ways to reduce bottlenecks like communication delays and uneven workload distribution.
    \item \textbf{Embracing Diverse Hardware:} Making the most of a mix of computing resources, from multi-core CPUs to GPUs and beyond.
    \item \textbf{Ensuring Reliability:} Keeping large-scale computations running smoothly even when parts of the system fail.
\end{itemize}

Our mission is to not only push forward the limits of high-performance computing but also to make sure that researchers and industry experts have the skills and tools needed to put these innovations to work.

\subsection{Project Scope and Objectives}

Exa-MA—and by extension, this deliverable—is all about taking a big-picture look at exascale computing while diving into practical details. Our main goals are to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Lay Out a Clear Roadmap:} Show how exascale methods can fit into today's workflows and align with industry needs.
    \item \textbf{Develop Cutting-Edge Training:} Create and share up-to-date training materials that showcase the latest in numerical methods and algorithms.
    \item \textbf{Support Smooth Adoption:} Make sure our training materials are easy to use and constantly updated so that users can confidently adopt exascale solutions.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{How We're Working}

We've broken our approach into three main phases to keep things clear and practical:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Step 1: Review and Plan} --- We start by looking at what training materials already exist, spotting gaps, and setting priorities.
    \item \textbf{Step 2: Build and Improve} --- Next, we create new tutorials and workshops while refining what's already available.
    \item \textbf{Step 3: Get Feedback and Iterate} --- Finally, we gather input from users and make ongoing updates to keep our training materials relevant and effective.
\end{itemize}
