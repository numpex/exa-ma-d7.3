\subsection{Context and Objectives}

The Exa-MA project (\textit{Méthodes et Algorithmes à l'Exascale}) is a central part of the national PEPR NumPEx program. Its goal is to drive breakthrough research and to develop software that brings exascale-level computing to many scientific and industrial fields. Exa-MA focuses on pushing the limits of numerical methods and algorithms while addressing key exascale challenges (B1--B13) such as reducing communication overhead, managing diverse hardware, ensuring resilience, and processing large data sets.

Within Exa-MA, \textbf{Work Package 7 (WP7)} ensures that these innovative exascale solutions are shared with the broader community. \textbf{Task 7.4 (Training)} is responsible for creating, organizing, and sharing training materials that help both newcomers and experienced users understand the latest methods and software tools.

\textbf{Deliverable D7.3} offers an \textit{initial} look at the training materials and plans for the project. In this document, you will find:
\begin{itemize}
    \item An outline of our overall \textit{training strategy} (see  \cref{sec:strategy}) aimed at building exascale computing skills.
    \item A snapshot of the \textit{current training materials} (see \cref{sec:current}), including tutorials, workshops, and academic collaborations.
    \item A description of our future plans to refine and expand these materials (see~\cref{sec:next-steps-software}).
\end{itemize}

This document will be updated at future milestones (M24, M36, etc.) as new developments and feedback are received.

\subsection{Background and Motivation}

The move toward exascale computing offers significant opportunities but also presents real challenges.  
Traditional numerical methods and algorithms often struggle with the scale and complexity of current systems.  
Exa-MA was initiated to address these difficulties by advancing both computer science and mathematical approaches.  
We focus on boosting performance by reducing bottlenecks such as communication delays and uneven workload distribution.  
We also embrace diverse hardware by leveraging a mix of computing resources, from multi-core CPUs to GPUs.  
Furthermore, we work to ensure reliability by designing methods that keep computations running smoothly even if parts of the system fail.

Our mission is to advance high-performance computing and to equip researchers and industry experts with the necessary skills and tools.

\subsection{Project Scope and Objectives}

Exa-MA—and this deliverable—takes a high-level look at exascale computing while providing practical training details. Our main objectives are to:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Present a Clear Roadmap:} Outline how exascale methods can be integrated into current workflows and meet industry needs.
    \item \textbf{Share Initial Training Materials:} Provide an overview of the training content and pilot initiatives currently available.
    \item \textbf{Facilitate Adoption:} Ensure that our training resources are accessible and set the groundwork for future updates based on user feedback.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{How We're Working}

Our approach is broken down into three phases:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Review and Plan:} We assess existing training materials, identify gaps, and set priorities.
    \item \textbf{Build and Improve:} We create new tutorials and workshops while refining what is already available.
    \item \textbf{Get Feedback and Iterate:} We collect user input and make continuous updates to keep our training materials relevant.
\end{itemize}
