\section*{Executive Summary}

Deliverable D7.3 provides an initial look at the training materials and activities within the Exa-MA project. It focuses on how Work Package 7 (\textit{WP7}), and especially Task 7.4 (Training), helps share knowledge and build skills in exascale methods and software. This document lays the groundwork for an evolving training strategy that will continue to grow throughout the Exa-MA project.

\paragraph{Purpose and Scope}
Starting at M12, Exa-MA has begun building and refining training resources that address the key challenges of exascale computing, such as new discretization techniques, efficient solvers, model reduction, large-scale optimization, and uncertainty quantification. This deliverable presents:
\begin{itemize}
    \item An overview of the \textbf{training strategy} along with the goals and delivery formats (Section~\cref{sec:strategy}).
    \item A summary of early \textbf{training activities} and the initial \textbf{synergy} with Master's and Doctoral programs (\cref{sec:current}).
    \item A detailed \textbf{inventory of software-specific training materials},~\cref{sec:software:training}, highlighting what's available now and what's planned for the future (\cref{sec:software:nextsteps}).
    \item A clear \textbf{roadmap} on how training efforts will evolve in upcoming milestones (M24, M36, etc.) (~\cref{sec:next-steps-software}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Key Achievements to Date}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Preliminary Tutorials and Workshops:} Several partners have already provided training materials for the key software in Exa-MA.
    \item \textbf{Academic Integration:} We've started working with Master's and PhD programs, ensuring that our research ties in smoothly with higher education in applied mathematics, scientific computing, and HPC.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future Directions}
In the next phase, Exa-MA will:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Enhance and Expand Training Materials:} We'll incorporate feedback, introduce advanced exascale techniques (like GPU acceleration and communication avoidance), and develop more tutorials for mini- and proxy-apps and benchmarking.
    \item \textbf{Document the Development Process:} We plan to offer clear guidelines, best practices, and co-design approaches to support reproducible, high-quality contributions across Work Packages.
    \item \textbf{Strengthen Outreach:} We'll work on course offerings, workshops, and hackathons in partnership with other PCs of NumPEx, HPC centers, and possibly industry stakeholders.
\end{itemize}

By following this roadmap, Exa-MA's training program will steadily equip researchers, students, and industry practitioners with the knowledge and tools needed to scale numerical methods and algorithms to exascale platforms. This deliverable will be updated at M24 and beyond, reflecting ongoing progress, feedback, and new developments in the field.
