\section*{Executive Summary}

Deliverable D7.3 outlines the initial status of training materials and activities within the Exa-MA project, focusing on how Work Package 7 (\textit{WP7})—and especially Task 7.4—supports knowledge transfer and skill-building for exascale methods and software. 
This document serves as the foundation for an evolving training strategy that will continue to develop over the course of Exa-MA.

\paragraph{Purpose and Scope}
Starting M12, Exa-MA begins building and consolidating training resources that address the core challenges of exascale computing, including novel discretization techniques, efficient solvers, model reduction methods, large-scale optimization, and uncertainty quantification. This deliverable presents:
\begin{itemize}
    \item An overview of the \textbf{training strategy}, goals, and delivery formats (Section~\ref{sec:strategy}).
    \item A summary of early \textbf{training activities} and initial \textbf{synergy} with Master/Doctoral programs (Section~\ref{sec:current}).
    \item A detailed \textbf{inventory of software-specific training materials} (Table~\ref{tab:software-training}), highlighting current availability and future plans (Section~\ref{sec:software-status}).
    \item A \textbf{roadmap} detailing how training efforts will evolve through subsequent milestones (M24, M36, etc.) (Section~\ref{sec:next-steps-software}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Key Achievements to Date}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Preliminary Tutorials and Workshops:} Several partners have provided training materials for some of the software identified for Exa-MA.
    \item \textbf{Academic Integration:} Partnerships with Master's and PhD programs have been initiated, ensuring alignment between Exa-MA research objectives and higher education curricula in applied mathematics, scientific computing, and HPC.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Future Directions}
During the next phase, Exa-MA will:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Enhance and Expand Training Materials:} Incorporate user feedback, add advanced exascale techniques (e.g., GPU acceleration, communication avoidance), and develop additional tutorials for mini- and proxy-apps and Benchmarking.
    \item \textbf{Document the Exa-MA Development Process:} Provide clear guidelines, best practices, and co-design approaches that enable reproducible, high-quality contributions across WPs.
    \item \textbf{Strengthen Academic and Industrial Outreach:} Develop  course offerings, workshops, and hackathons, partnering with other PCs of NumPEx as well as HPC centers and possibly industry stakeholders.
\end{itemize}

By following this roadmap, Exa-MA's training program will systematically equip researchers, students, and industry practitioners with the tools and knowledge required to scale numerical methods and algorithms to upcoming exascale platforms. 
The deliverable will be updated at M24 and beyond to reflect ongoing developments, ensuring that the training content remains current with scientific and technological progress throughout the Exa-MA project.
