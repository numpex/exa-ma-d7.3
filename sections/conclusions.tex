\chapter{Conclusions}
\label{chap:conclusions}

\section*{Summary}
In this deliverable, we've shared an overview of Exa-MA's training strategy and given a snapshot of our current training activities, software-specific materials, and plans for future improvements. 
Key points include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The progress we've made in creating an accessible training framework for exascale methods and software.
    \item Early successes with tutorials, workshops, and academic collaborations.
    \item Clear next steps to refresh our existing materials and develop new content that meets the changing needs of HPC and exascale computing.
\end{itemize}
These insights lay the foundation for a training ecosystem that supports both newcomers and seasoned users in tackling the challenges of exascale computing.

\section*{Call to Action}
We invite all our stakeholders—researchers, educators, HPC practitioners, and industry partners—to join us in shaping and benefiting from our training initiatives. 
The feedback and active participation are key to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Refining our training materials based on real-world experience.
    \item Identifying fresh topics and new ways to deliver content that suit different audiences.
    \item Expanding our network of academic and industrial partners to make our training even richer.
\end{itemize}
We welcome our stakeholders to join our effort in building a strong, community-driven training program that equips users to meet the challenges and seize the opportunities of exascale computing.

\section*{Commitment to Building HPC Skills}
Exa-MA is fully committed to developing a skilled and resilient workforce for the exascale era. 
Our ongoing efforts to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Update and enhance training materials,
    \item Document best practices and our development process, and
    \item Engage the community through workshops, webinars, and hands-on labs,
\end{itemize}
ensure that our training program is up to date with the latest in HPC technology.
