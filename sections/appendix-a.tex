\section{Appendix A: Sample Form for Exa-MA Master Track Information Request}

Below is the set of questions for requesting information about the Master track from the responsible manager. 

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Name of the Master Track:} \\
    \emph{(e.g., Master in Computational Science and Engineering)}
    
    \item \textbf{Courses Related to Exa-MA/HPC and Their Levels:} \\
    \emph{(Please list course titles and indicate whether they are offered at Master 1 or Master 2 level. For example: "Advanced Numerical Methods (Master 2), HPC Fundamentals (Master 1)")}
    
    \item \textbf{Training Format:} \\
    \emph{Please indicate whether the track is project-based, includes internships, or both.} \\
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Project-based track
        \item Includes internships
        \item Both project-based and internships
        \item Other (please specify)
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Link to the Master Track Information:} \\
    \emph{(Provide the URL to the webpage or document with further details on your Master track)}
    
    \item \textbf{Contact Preferences:} \\
    \emph{Would you like to receive requests/emails from Exa-MA regarding internships or PhD proposals?} \\
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Yes
        \item No
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Additional Training Interest:} \\
    \emph{Would you or your colleagues/teaching staff be interested in additional training on HPC and Exascale methods and algorithms?} \\
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Yes, definitely
        \item Maybe, need more information
        \item No, not at the moment
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}


\vspace{1em}
\noindent \textbf{Data Protection Notice (RGPD):} \\
Your responses will be processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and our internal data protection policies. 
All personal data provided in this form will be kept confidential and used solely for the purpose of coordinating Exa-MA training initiatives.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent Thank you for providing this information.
Your input is highly valued and will help us better tailor our training activities.
